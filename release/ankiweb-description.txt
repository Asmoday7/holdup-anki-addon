HoldUp — Extra practice alongside FSRS
Version 1.0.0 — free and open source

HoldUp adds a new button to Anki for extra practice at custom intervals, alongside your normal Anki/FSRS reviews.

https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Asmoday7/holdup-anki-addon/main/release/assets/example-08-ready-badge.png
A small addition to your deck list: the green clock badge shows when HoldUp practice is ready. Open the deck as usual to study it.

Want another look at a card before Anki brings it back? HoldUp schedules an extra appearance at a time you choose, alongside your normal Anki reviews.

HOW IT WORKS, STEP BY STEP

These screenshots show Japanese sentence cards. HoldUp can also be used with other subjects.

1. Choose when you want another look
Use the … menu to choose 1 second to 24 hours, or Next Anki study day using Anki's day-start setting. This setting applies to subsequent HoldUp presses. The screenshots use a short interval to demonstrate the flow.
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Asmoday7/holdup-anki-addon/main/release/assets/example-05-interval.png

2. Show the answer and press HoldUp
Schedule an extra appearance of the card at your chosen interval.
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Asmoday7/holdup-anki-addon/main/release/assets/example-01-normal-review.png

3. Choose your normal Anki rating
The checkmark and increased waiting count confirm that extra practice is scheduled. The original stays open: choose Again, Hard, Good or Easy to finish grading it as usual.
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Asmoday7/holdup-anki-addon/main/release/assets/example-02-holdup-scheduled.png

4. See when extra practice is ready
When its time arrives, a green clock badge appears next to the deck name on the Decks screen. It counts ready HoldUp appearances; the native Anki counts remain visible alongside it.
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Asmoday7/holdup-anki-addon/main/release/assets/example-07-deck-list.png

5. Open the deck and continue studying
The deck overview shows both waiting and ready appearances, with ready practice included in the green review count. Continue studying returns to the same reviewer.
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Asmoday7/holdup-anki-addon/main/release/assets/example-06-deck-overview.png

6. The card returns for extra practice
Ready appearances join the same reviewer. The HoldUp label identifies practice without an FSRS rating. Press Show Answer as usual.
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Asmoday7/holdup-anki-addon/main/release/assets/example-03-practice-question.png

7. Repeat or finish this extra appearance
Again in… reschedules only this appearance; Done finishes it. These actions do not submit a normal FSRS rating.
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Asmoday7/holdup-anki-addon/main/release/assets/example-04-practice-answer.png

A FEW MORE DETAILS

HoldUp does not choose a normal rating for you. The original keeps its normal Anki schedule, FSRS history and daily new-card accounting. Extra practice does not submit an FSRS rating, although it can affect what you remember on your next normal review.

- Each new HoldUp press adds a separate extra appearance; older appointments keep their times.
- Use normal Undo/Redo for appointments, repeats and completion.
- English and Russian are included in one package and follow Anki's language.

COMPATIBILITY

Desktop Anki 26.09 and 26.09.3 on Windows and Linux. macOS has not been verified.
First-release support is one desktop installation per collection. Full collection exports and Anki backups preserve the extra queue; deck .apkg export does not transfer the complete queue. Live multi-device sync/conflict resolution is unverified.
AnkiDroid and AnkiMobile do not run desktop Python add-ons.
