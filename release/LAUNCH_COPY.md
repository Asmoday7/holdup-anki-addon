## English

I've released **HoldUp 1.0.0**, a free add-on for Anki.

Want another look at a card at a time you choose, alongside your normal review
schedule? Show the answer, press HoldUp, then give the original your normal
Anki/FSRS rating. The extra appearance joins the same reviewer without submitting
its own FSRS rating.

Choose from one second to 24 hours, or the next Anki study day. Undo/Redo is
supported, and English/Russian interfaces are included in one package.
Tested with desktop Anki 26.09 and 26.09.3 on Windows and Linux.

[Download HoldUp 1.0.0](https://github.com/Asmoday7/holdup-anki-addon/releases/tag/v1.0.0)
