HoldUp — Extra practice alongside FSRS

HoldUp adds a new button to Anki for extra practice at custom intervals, alongside your normal Anki/FSRS reviews.

Version 1.0.0 — free and open source.

Want another look at a card before Anki brings it back? HoldUp schedules an extra appearance at a time you choose, alongside your normal Anki reviews.

  1. Show the answer and press HoldUp.
  2. Give the original your normal Again, Hard, Good or Easy rating.
  3. The extra appearance becomes ready at your chosen time and joins the same reviewer.

After pressing HoldUp: a checkmark and the waiting count confirm that extra practice is scheduled. The original stays open, with the normal Anki rating buttons still available. Choose one to finish grading the original.

HoldUp does not choose a normal rating for you. The original keeps its normal Anki schedule, FSRS history and daily new-card accounting. Extra practice does not submit an FSRS rating, although it can affect what you remember on your next normal review.

  • Choose 1 second to 24 hours, or Next Anki study day using Anki's day-start setting.
  • Each new HoldUp press adds a separate extra appearance; older appointments keep their times.
  • Again in… reschedules the appearance on screen; Done finishes only that appearance.
  • Use normal Undo/Redo for appointments, repeats and completion.
  • See ready appearances in the green review count and a clock badge on the deck list.
  • English and Russian are included in one package and follow Anki's language.

See the workflow

These screenshots show Japanese sentence cards. HoldUp can also be used with other subjects.

1. The original card: normal Anki ratings and HoldUp
Use HoldUp to schedule extra practice at your preferred interval.

2. HoldUp pressed: now choose a normal Anki rating
The checkmark and increased waiting count confirm the new appointment. The four normal rating buttons remain available: choose one to finish grading the original.

3. Extra practice in the same reviewer
When an appearance is ready, it uses the same study window. The HoldUp label identifies practice without an FSRS rating.

4. Repeat or finish this extra appearance
Again in… reschedules only this appearance; Done finishes it. These actions do not submit a normal FSRS rating.

5. Choose an interval or the next Anki study day
The examples use a short interval to demonstrate the flow. Choose from 1 second to 24 hours, or use the next day-start boundary in Anki preferences.

6. See what is waiting and what is ready
The deck overview shows both waiting and ready appearances. Continue studying returns to the same reviewer.

7. Find ready practice on the Decks screen
Green clock badges show ready HoldUp appearances next to the deck names. The native Anki counts remain visible alongside them.

A closer look at the ready badge
The badge counts extra appearances. Open the deck by its normal name to continue studying.

Tested compatibility: desktop Anki 26.09 and 26.09.3 on Windows and Linux. macOS has not been verified. First-release support is one desktop installation per collection. Full collection exports and Anki backups preserve the extra queue; deck .apkg export does not transfer the complete queue. Live multi-device sync/conflict resolution is unverified. AnkiDroid and AnkiMobile do not run desktop Python add-ons.
