HoldUp — Build your own extra review queue in one click

HoldUp builds a parallel review queue on the fly, separate from your normal Anki/FSRS schedule.

Version 1.0.0 — free and open source.

The little green badge means there's HoldUp practice ready in that deck. Open the deck as usual to study it.

Under the hood, it's dead simple: set your interval once, then hit HoldUp to add an extra appearance of the card to your own queue. Rate the original normally — Again, Hard, Good or Easy — letting Anki/FSRS schedule its usual interval, say, two days. Adding a HoldUp appearance doesn't answer or reschedule the original.

Wanting a quick re-check tonight doesn't mean I'm unhappy with the algorithm. For example, I might remember a kanji perfectly fine and be totally okay with a two-day gap, but still have this random urge to double-check one of its components or radicals tonight, just to be safe…

This add-on lets you scratch that itch right away. With your interval already set, queue up that extra review in one click, keep your flow, and move on. When it's ready, it joins the green review count and shows up when you study that deck.

Sure, you could put together something similar with flags or filtered decks. For me, that setup gets in the way. I wanted to build that parallel queue directly during reviews without stopping to organise it manually.

It's not meant to improve FSRS predictions… it's just a fast shortcut for optional extra practice.

Extra practice doesn't submit FSRS ratings, so FSRS doesn't know about those extra looks. They can still affect what you remember at your next normal review.

How it works, step by step

These screenshots show Japanese sentence cards. HoldUp can also be used with other subjects.

1. Choose when you want another look
Use the … menu to choose 1 second to 24 hours, or Next Anki study day using Anki's day-start setting. This setting applies to subsequent HoldUp presses. The screenshots use a short interval to demonstrate the flow.

2. Show the answer and press HoldUp
Schedule an extra appearance of the card at your chosen interval.

3. Choose your normal Anki rating
The checkmark and increased waiting count confirm that extra practice is scheduled. The original stays open: choose Again, Hard, Good or Easy to finish grading it as usual.

4. See when extra practice is ready
When its time arrives, a green clock badge appears next to the deck name on the Decks screen. It counts ready HoldUp appearances; the native Anki counts remain visible alongside it.

5. Open the deck and continue studying
The deck overview shows both waiting and ready appearances, with ready practice included in the green review count. Continue studying returns to the same reviewer.

6. The card returns for extra practice
Ready appearances join the same reviewer. The HoldUp label identifies practice without an FSRS rating. Press Show Answer as usual.

7. Repeat or finish this extra appearance
Again in… reschedules only this appearance; Done finishes it. These actions do not submit a normal FSRS rating.

A few more details

Your normal Anki rating handles the original card's schedule, FSRS history and daily new-card accounting.

  • Each new HoldUp press adds a separate extra appearance; older appointments keep their times.
  • Use normal Undo/Redo for appointments, repeats and completion.
  • English and Russian are included in one package and follow Anki's language.

Tested compatibility: desktop Anki 26.09 and 26.09.3 on Windows and Linux. macOS has not been verified. First-release support is one desktop installation per collection. Full collection exports and Anki backups preserve the extra queue; deck .apkg export does not transfer the complete queue. Live multi-device sync/conflict resolution is unverified. AnkiDroid and AnkiMobile do not run desktop Python add-ons.
